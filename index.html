Play on a HomePod or Apple TV

Open this page in Safari on iPhone or iPad, start a station, then tap AirPlay and choose the speaker. A web app can’t appear in the Home app or CarPlay, but AirPlay sends the sound to any HomePod or Apple TV on the same Wi‑Fi.

On a Mac, the AirPlay button in Safari’s media controls also works.
